PHASE 03

Barhoumatic Revision Drops

A 60-second Aura Loop

One student, one minute, and the whole system at work. This is the full Aura Decathlon format we are building toward.

1 · ENTRY

Tap in

The student taps their Bio-Aura Band on a station. The HoumaHype app authenticates them and opens their profile.

2 · CHALLENGE

Move and think

On the Hype-Bike, the student keeps their heart rate up to power the 4K LED wall. A physics prompt is built from their own live RPM and power.

3 · INSIGHT

Gaps found

If the answer is wrong, the system records the exact concept behind the mistake and drops a revision video into the student’s wallet.

4 · RESULT

Data for teachers

The school receives a Skill-Map report showing which concepts the class found hardest, so teaching can be targeted the next day.

The technology roadmap

The Aura Decathlon hardware is in development. Band, pod and server are designed to work as one system, with no dependence on school Wi-Fi or the internet.

Wearable

Bio-Aura Band

Heart rate, HRV, SpO₂, skin response and motion sensing at 50 Hz. Waterproof, sand-proof and built for 96 hours of use.

Station

Aura-Pod Activity Dock

A collision-safe riser column at hand height, with a rugged 12.9" screen, NFC tap validation and a status ring.

Network

Autonomous Aura Mesh

A private Wi-Fi 6E network covering a stadium-sized field. Every pod extends the signal so data syncs instantly.

Command

Mobile Edge Server

An AI-accelerated server inside the tour truck processes live data and drives the exterior leaderboard wall.
